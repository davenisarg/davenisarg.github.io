Designed optimization models at Asurion for field technician routing and job scheduling.

The system uses geospatial data to minimize drive time between service appointments, clusters nearby jobs to create efficient daily routes, and dynamically adjusts schedules based on real-time demand. Also replaced manual Excel-based demand planning with automated time-series forecasting.

**Impact:**
- Drive time reduced by 30% (9 min/job)
- 1.1 additional jobs per technician per day
- $1-1.5M in annual cost savings
- Eliminated manual Excel-based planning workflows

**Tech:** Geospatial optimization, clustering algorithms, time-series forecasting, Python
