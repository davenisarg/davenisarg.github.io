At Asurion, I designed geospatial optimization and real-time job clustering models for field technician routing and scheduling—replacing manual Excel-based planning with automated, data-driven operations.

## The Problem

Field technicians were spending too much time driving between jobs and not enough time on repairs. Route planning was done manually in spreadsheets, demand forecasting was guesswork, and there was no systematic way to cluster nearby jobs into efficient daily routes. The inefficiency translated directly into lost revenue and higher operating costs.

## What I Built

**Route Optimization:** Uses geospatial data to minimize drive time between service appointments. Clusters nearby jobs to create efficient daily routes and dynamically adjusts schedules based on real-time demand, cancellations, and technician availability.

**Demand Forecasting:** Automated time-series forecasting models that predict service demand by region, category, and time window. This replaced the manual Excel-based planning process entirely, improving accuracy and eliminating hours of weekly planning overhead.

## Impact

- **Drive time reduced by 30%** (9 minutes saved per job)
- **1.1 additional jobs per technician per day**
- **$1-1.5M in annual cost savings**
- Eliminated manual Excel-based demand planning workflows

## Tech Stack

Geospatial optimization, clustering algorithms, time-series forecasting, Python, AWS
