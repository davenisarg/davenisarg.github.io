Led R&D at Asurion on adapting open-source LLMs for enterprise client use cases.

Fine-tuned LLaMA and Mistral models using LoRA and self-instruct pipelines on domain-specific data. Built synthetic data generation workflows to augment limited labeled datasets. Developed RAG-based prototypes that connect LLMs to internal knowledge bases for grounded, accurate responses.

Delivered working prototypes for HP, Amazon, and internal service channels. This work established the technical playbook for how Asurion evaluates, customizes, and deploys LLMs across its product lines.

**Tech:** LLaMA, Mistral, LoRA, self-instruct, RAG, synthetic data generation, Hugging Face
