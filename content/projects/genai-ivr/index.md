Built a Generative AI-powered interactive voice response system at Asurion that automates insurance claims processing from intake through resolution.

The system uses fine-tuned LLMs and NLP to understand customer intent, classify claim types, and route or resolve without human intervention. Designed for multi-tenant SaaS deployment, it now serves enterprise clients including Amazon and McAfee.

**Impact:** $5-10M in annual operating cost reduction.

**Tech:** LLM fine-tuning (LoRA), NLP, multi-tenant SaaS architecture, real-time inference
