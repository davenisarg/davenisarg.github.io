Research project under DARPA's Explainable AI program, funded by the U.S. Department of Defense at Michigan Technological University.

Developed deep neural networks using GoogLeNet, AlexNet, and ResNet architectures on ImageNet. Retrained Google's Inception classifier and achieved 96-98% top-5 accuracy. Investigated Choquet Integral-based computational intelligence methods to generate human-interpretable explanations for model predictions.

Studied how deep image classifiers fail under real-world input variations. Showed that standard classifiers degrade significantly under image transformations that are trivial for humans, highlighting gaps in model robustness for safety-critical deployment.

**Outcomes:**
- Published: "The Unreasonable Ineptitude of Deep Image Classification Networks" at [35th ICML Workshop](https://github.com/davenisarg/unreasonable)
- Advisor: Dr. Timothy Havens, Director of ICC Data Sciences

**Tech:** TensorFlow, CNNs (GoogLeNet, ResNet-51, ResNet-101), HPC infrastructure, transfer learning
