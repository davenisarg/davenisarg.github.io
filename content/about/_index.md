## The Short Version

Data Scientist with 7+ years building production AI/ML systems across healthcare, telecom, and retail. I work on LLM fine-tuning, conversational AI, NLP, computer vision, and optimization problems. I care about shipping things that work and making a measurable difference.

Currently at Asurion, where my work has driven $10M+ in annual savings across GenAI-powered IVR systems, NER-based automation, and geospatial optimization.

Before that, I built the ML and computer vision pipeline for an FDA-regulated Class II diagnostic device at HueDx. That work led to a granted patent and a publication in Frontiers in Chemistry.

I started in research at Michigan Tech, working on DARPA's Explainable AI program under the U.S. Department of Defense. Co-authored a paper on CNN robustness presented at the 35th ICML Workshop.

## Beyond Work

I'm most at home under open skies. Whether it's hiking through wild trails, camping under the stars, or just quietly watching the moon, that's where I recharge.

After hours, I trade datasets for cameras. I photograph wildlife, the moon, stars, and the faces that catch my eye. There's something about finding the right frame that feels a lot like finding the right feature in a dataset, except the lighting is harder to control.

I write poetry when inspiration hits, travel to get lost in new cultures, and hunt for hidden food gems wherever I go. I'm also a serious cook. The kind who cares about the history behind a dish as much as the flavor. Ask me about the origin of any regional cuisine and I'll probably talk your ear off.
