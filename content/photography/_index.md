I'm most at home under open skies. Whether it's hiking through wild trails, camping under the stars, chasing adventures, or just quietly getting lost in the night watching the moon.

By day, I'm a data scientist. After hours, I chase light. I photograph wildlife, the moon, stars, and the faces that catch my eye. There's something about finding the right frame that keeps pulling me back.

Traveling, getting lost in new cultures, finding hidden food gems, and cooking up a storm when I get home. I care about the history behind a dish as much as the flavor.

I also write poetry when inspiration hits. Some things need words instead of pixels.
